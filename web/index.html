Multimodal research demo

Text signal + facial-expression signal, side by side.

Analyze short English/Hindi/Hinglish text with a transparent multilingual lexicon and optionally run a browser facial-expression model on your webcam. The demo keeps the two signals separate and labels them as model outputs—not as ground truth about a person's inner feelings.
